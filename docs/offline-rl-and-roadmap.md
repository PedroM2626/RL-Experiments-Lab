### 6.1. Offline RL Empirical Results on Bossfight (`results/offline_rl_results.json`)

Evaluated four canonical offline RL paradigms on an offline dataset of 100,000 transitions (1,804 episodes) collected on `bossfight` under mixed exploratory and tactical behavior. All models share the canonical NatureCNN visual feature encoder backbone. Evaluation was conducted across 20 stochastic unseen episodes (`num_levels=0`, evaluation seed `1042`), with results persisted in `results/offline_rl_results.json`:

| Method | Paradigm | Mean Return ± Std | 95% Student's $t$ CI | Empirical Behavior |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| **`DT`** (Decision Transformer) | Sequence Modeling ($R_t$-conditioned) | **0.60 ± 2.46** | **[-0.55, 1.75]** | Conditioned on high RTG; damages boss |
| `BC` (Behavioral Cloning) | Supervised Policy Learning | 0.00 ± 0.00 | [0.00, 0.00] | Fails to avoid boss projectiles |
| `IQL` (Implicit Q-Learning) | In-Sample Expectile Value + AWR | 0.00 ± 0.00 | [0.00, 0.00] | Value collapse on pixel actions |
| `CQL` (Conservative Q-Learning)| Conservative Lower-Bound Q-Penalty | 0.00 ± 0.00 | [0.00, 0.00] | Over-conservative under sparse rewards |

> **Scientific Insight:** In pixel-observation discrete control with sparse rewards and high adversary lethality (`bossfight`), autoregressive sequence modeling conditioned on desired future returns (`Decision Transformer`) successfully discovered positive reward trajectories ($0.60$ mean return), outperforming all three value-based and behavioral cloning baselines which collapsed to $0.00$ due to distributional shift and compounding error under unguided rollouts.

Transitions substitute for levels (`1×1024`) — a 256-transition update needs ~4 distinct levels, or more transitions to compensate. **Then the (iv) dose** (§3.6.5, 14/10/2026, 10 runs, 15.0 GPU-minutes): `2×128` dies 0/5, `8×32` ends 3/5 — at fixed batch the levels factor has a cliff between 2 and 4 (below it: `14/14` seeds dead across `1×256`/`2×128`/`repeat4`/`ent_coef`; at or above: `17/22` survive), and transitions substitute for levels (`1×1024`, ~2 levels, survives 2/2) — the account is now quantitative: a 256-transition update needs ~4 distinct levels, or more transitions to compensate. **Then the (iii) pilot** (§3.6.6, 14/10/2026: all 16 arms on `starpilot`, seed 42, `16×256`, 53.7 cell-minutes): the rescue generalizes — 13/16 arms respond (TV `0.23–0.46`), returns spread `1.00–6.40`, and the paired ordering inverts — the old bottom four average `4.58` against the old top four's `1.70`, `classic` `0.50 → 5.20`. The measured rate revises the full 240-cell grid to **~13.4 GPU-hours**, not ~40. **Then the (iii) grid** (§3.6.7: all `240` cells at `16×256`, 3h22m wall): the rescue generalizes across the comparison, the archived top-3 falls to **12th–14th**, and the return ordering *still* does not track visual conditioning — the new top-2 (`vit` `1.907`, `impoola` `1.707`) are the two least-responsive arms (1/15 and 3/15 cells), `mlp_vector` is 7th and third-least responsive (its leadership does not survive the fix), while the response measure separates the front ends where return does not (`cbam`/`classic`/`spatial`/`c_aug_color` read on 15/15 cells; `vit`/`impoola`/`mlp_vector` on 1–3); `ae`≡`recon` and `contrastive`≡`c_aug_noise` stay identical across all 45 cells, and `vae`'s `bossfight`/seed-42 `NaN` recurs under the new recipe (~5.9k steps) — a (`vae`, `bossfight`) property, not a rollout effect. **Closing verdict:** return at this budget is not a measure of the visual front end under either recipe; the rankings could not have been repaired by re-running them, and the response table — which this item built — is the architecture result. Two incidental defects fell out along the way: `vae` goes `NaN` mid-training on `bossfight`/seed `42` and reaches a `7.2e13` latent on `dodgeball`, and `ae`/`recon` are the same network (14 distinct front ends, not 16). |
